    01 · Why AeroHome

    Your cameras record crimes. They don't prevent them.

    Fixed cameras have fixed blind spots, and alarms only sound after someone is already inside. AeroHome was built on one idea: stop the break-in before it starts.

    TRADITIONAL SECURITY

    Passive. Reactive. Blind spots.

    • Fixed cameras watch the same angles an intruder learns in minutes
    • The alarm sounds after the door or window is breached
    • Unverified motion alerts you learn to ignore
    • Footage becomes evidence of a crime that already happened
    AEROHOME ACTIVE SECURITY

    Mobile. Verified. Deterrent.

    • The drone investigates any sound or sensor trigger, anywhere on the property
    • AI verifies a real person before you're ever woken up
    • Spotlight, voice challenge and siren confront intruders at the fence line
    • Verified live video gets priority emergency dispatch
    02 · The response ladder

    From first sound to all-clear, in six steps.

    Every event climbs the same ladder, and most stop at step four. Run one and watch it climb.

    DETECTINVESTIGATEVERIFYDETERESCALATEDOCUMENT

    ILLUSTRATION · SIMULATED EVENT · NOT LIVE FOOTAGE

    1. Detect

      A sensor, a sound or a camera detection — a person at the fence at 2 a.m. — creates a candidate event.

    2. Investigate

      Sentinel launches to the exact zone. Guardian rolls to the room. Eyes on target in seconds.

    3. Verify

      On-device AI confirms person, vehicle, animal or package. Raccoons never wake you. People do.

    4. Deter

      The spotlight locks on. A voice challenge announces police contact. Most intruders abandon the attempt right here.

    5. Escalate

      Live verified video to you and our 24/7 monitoring agents. One-tap 911 with your address and a live-feed link.

    6. Document

      A multi-angle 4K and thermal record, packaged automatically for police and your insurance claim.

    06 · Privacy by design

    Your footage. Your rules. Period.

    • AI runs in your home, not our cloud.Threat verification happens on the Hub. Footage is end-to-end encrypted.
    • Police get what they need, when you say so.One tap calls 911 with your address and a live-feed link, and a documented evidence package is ready for responding officers. Otherwise your footage stays private, released only by you or under a lawful order.
    • Bedrooms are off-limits by default.Guardian's no-go zones are enforced on the device, and its camera shutter is physical and visible.
    • Neighbors stay out of frame.Sentinel never crosses your property line, and anything beyond it is blurred at capture.
