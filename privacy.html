Privacy Policy

What AeroHome collects through this website and the waitlist, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated · 1 Oct 2026Website + waitlist
ON THIS PAGE
    Template notice. This policy is a starting point prepared for the AeroHome website. It has not been reviewed by an attorney. Have counsel review it, and confirm the company's legal name and address, before relying on it. A separate privacy notice will govern AeroHome devices and the app.

    This policy explains what information AeroHome (“AeroHome”, “we”, “us”) collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the website and the waitlist only. It does not cover the AeroHome hardware, the app or monitoring services, which will have their own privacy notice when they become available.

    1. Information you give us

    When you join the waitlist or contact us, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, ZIP code, metro area, and the hardware and plan you are interested in, plus anything you choose to write to us.

    2. Information collected automatically

    Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed and the time of your visit. We use it to keep the site running and secure. The interactive illustration and the “Build your setup” calculator run entirely in your browser; the values you enter there are not sent to us.

    3. How we use your information

    • To manage the waitlist and contact you about the pilot program, installation and founder pricing
    • To plan where and when to launch, using the metro and ZIP code information you provide
    • To answer your questions
    • To keep the website secure and working

    We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

    4. Who we share it with

    We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and send email, under contracts that limit their use of it, and where required by law. If AeroHome is involved in a merger, financing or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

    5. Law enforcement

    We support law enforcement. We share information with police when you direct us to, and we comply with valid legal process such as a warrant, subpoena or court order. Where the law allows, we will let you know.

    6. How long we keep it

    We keep waitlist information until the pilot program in your area opens and for a reasonable period afterward, or until you ask us to delete it.

    7. Your choices and rights

    You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to remove you from the waitlist, at any time by emailing info@aeroassist.us. Every email we send includes a way to opt out. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law, and we will honor them.

    8. Security

    We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

    9. Children

    This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

    10. Changes

    We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

    11. Contact

    Questions about privacy: info@aeroassist.us.
